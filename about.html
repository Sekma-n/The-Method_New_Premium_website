About The Method

Created with intention.

A cinematic lifestyle platform exploring modern African living through conversation, wine, food, creativity, and intentional experiences.

Our Manifesto

Live with intention.

We believe life should be experienced with intention. In a world moving faster than ever, The Method exists as a return to presence — to conversation, to culture, to beauty, to meaningful experiences shared around tables, glasses, music, ideas, and people.

We are inspired by the art of living well. Not loudly. Not performatively. But intentionally.

We believe wine has never only been about wine. It has always been about connection. About stories. About slowing down long enough to truly experience people, spaces, culture, and self.

— Wine. Culture. Intentional Living.

Founder

Luyanda Dlamini

Luyanda Dlamini is the founder and host of The Method — a platform born from a deep desire to document and celebrate intentional living, modern African culture, and the people shaping it.

With a background in media, culture, and hospitality, Luyanda created The Method as a space where conversations are slow, experiences are curated, and stories are told with cinematic depth.

She believes that the most meaningful moments happen around a table — with good wine, great company, and the willingness to be fully present.

— Luyanda Dlamini
